# CUMCM 2026 official baseline

Last verified: 2026-09-09. Recheck the official pages before a live submission because current notices and regional requirements may change.

## Source priority

1. Current national rules and paper-format specification.
2. Current regional and school notices.
3. User-selected template and output preferences.
4. Common practice or optional style recommendations.

Never present an optional style choice as a national requirement.

## National paper-format baseline

Official source: <https://www.mcm.edu.cn/html_cn/node/4cd596519c9eb9fbd866398f6df0caa3.html>

- Paper copy: white A4; margins at least 2.5 cm on all sides; bind on the left.
- Paper copy page order: commitment page, numbering-only page, abstract page, then body.
- The abstract page contains title, abstract, and keywords and should normally fit one page.
- Page numbering begins on the abstract page at `1`, centered in the footer.
- Do not include a table of contents. The body must not exceed 30 pages; appendices are outside that limit.
- The appendix includes a support-material file list and all complete runnable source code or interactive software commands. If no program was used, state that explicitly.
- Abstract, body, and appendix must contain no participant, school, or region identity.
- Every borrowed public result must be cited in the body and listed in the references.
- National rules do not prescribe a universal font, size, line spacing, or color. Regional rules may add requirements.
- Electronic paper: one PDF or Word file, PDF recommended, no compression, at most 20 MB. It excludes commitment/number pages, so page 1 is the abstract page.
- Electronic and paper versions must match, including the appendix.
- Support materials: one ZIP or RAR, at most 20 MB, containing all necessary source programs, independently collected data, large intermediate results, and other evidence. Do not include the contest-provided raw data again unless a current notice requires it.

## Competition and communication baseline

Official source: <https://www.mcm.edu.cn/html_cn/node/9d8e511fe7a1447b35f53a82c908e2e0.html>

- Each team completes the work independently.
- During the competition, no discussion of the current problem with people outside the team, including instructors.
- During the competition, do not browse, publish, or discuss current-problem content on GitHub, forums, groups, livestreams, or other communication platforms.
- Public sources may be used only within the current rules and must be cited when incorporated.

## AI-use baseline

Official source: <https://www.mcm.edu.cn/html_en/node/532f38e63cfa84145697e2678575ba0a.html>

- AI tools are optional assistants. Core modeling and analysis remain team-led.
- Every adopted AI output is manually reviewed and verified.
- Every paper contains an AI tool usage declaration before the references, whether AI was used or not.
- If AI was used, the support package contains `AI工具使用详情.pdf` with tool/model, purpose/stage, prompting and use process, and adoption/manual modification/verification details. Pure language polishing does not require the last category itemized.
- Concealing AI use, making a false declaration, or submitting AI-generated core modeling without necessary review and verification can revoke award eligibility.

## Profile boundary

The bundled Word/LaTeX templates may choose fonts, colors, table styles, chapter order, and other visual conventions. Apply those as template defaults, not official national rules. If a regional notice conflicts with the template, update the working copy and record the deviation.
